Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete a project? +
The duration of a project depends on its complexity, features, and specific requirements. Simple websites or landing pages usually take around 3 to 5 days to complete. More advanced projects, such as custom web applications or feature-rich platforms, may take between 7 to 14 days or even longer. We always provide a clear timeline after analyzing your requirements and keep you updated throughout the development process.
Do you provide support? +
Yes, we provide reliable and professional support after the project is delivered. This includes fixing bugs, answering your questions, and guiding you on how to manage your website. Free support is available for a limited period, and we also offer extended support packages for long-term maintenance, updates, and performance improvements.
Is the website responsive? +
Absolutely. All our websites are fully responsive and designed to work seamlessly on all devices, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. We carefully test each project on different screen sizes to ensure a smooth user experience, fast loading speed, and modern design standards across all platforms.
How is the cost calculated? +
The cost of a project is determined based on several factors such as the number of features, design complexity, level of customization, and development time required. After discussing your needs, we provide a transparent and detailed quotation so you clearly understand what you are paying for. Our goal is to deliver high-quality work at a fair and reasonable price.
